Data handling

Controlled pilot summary · Last updated: 8 September 2026

Current pilot approach

  • The private Studio is access controlled.
  • The current mailbox workflow is designed to process messages that have been explicitly marked for Property Dossier processing rather than scanning an entire inbox automatically.
  • Property facts extracted from an email remain subject to agent review.
  • Official or document checks are kept separate from vendor or landlord supplied information.
  • The Studio does not present itself as a legal compliance verifier.

For demonstrations

Use fictional, anonymised or redacted examples wherever possible. Do not use special category data, passwords, bank details or information that is not needed to demonstrate the property intake workflow.

For a live agency pilot

Before a live pilot with genuine customer information, the parties should agree the authorised mailbox, users, data scope, retention, deletion, security responsibilities and any required contractual data-processing terms.
