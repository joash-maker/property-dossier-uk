Terms

Pilot website terms · Last updated: 8 September 2026

What the Studio does

Property Dossier Studio helps organise property instruction information, record source evidence, highlight missing items and prepare an internal CRM handoff.

What it does not do

The Studio does not replace an estate agency CRM, legal advice, professional judgement, AML checks, conveyancing, or an agency's legal and regulatory responsibilities. It does not certify that a property is legally or regulatorily compliant.

Pilot use

Access is currently provided only for controlled demonstrations and agreed pilot work. Features may change while the product is being tested. A commercial client engagement may require separate written terms covering scope, data handling, support and liability.
