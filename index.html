The admin problem

The information is usually there. It just arrives in the wrong shape.

A vendor or landlord rarely sends a perfect CRM record. Details arrive across emails, follow-ups, attachments and conversations. Someone still has to read it, decide what matters, copy it across, chase the gaps and keep the record straight.

Information arrives in stages

Rent today. EPC tomorrow. Access instructions later. The property record gets patched together over time.

Staff re-key the same facts

Details are copied from email into CRM fields, notes, portal records and internal checklists.

Missing details are easy to overlook

Long emails can contain twenty useful facts. A few missed items become tomorrow’s chasing.

FAQ

The obvious questions.

We already have a CRM. Why would we need this?

Because the Studio handles the unstructured information before the CRM record is ready. It is designed to reduce the reading, copying, checking and chasing that still happens around the CRM.

Does it verify legal compliance?

No. It records what has been supplied, what source it came from and what the agent has reviewed. Official, legal and document checks remain separate and the agency keeps responsibility for professional judgement.

Does it only work with Gmail?

No. The product is designed around a connected company mailbox, not a specific email provider. The current controlled prototype uses a marked-email workflow, and future agency pilots can use the appropriate mailbox connection for that organisation.

Can it handle lettings and property management?

Yes. The current workflow supports lettings information such as deposit, furnishing, availability, gas safety, EICR, maintenance authority, stopcock, fuse board and access instructions.

Is this only for the UK?

The current pilot is configured for England. Country-specific property workflows can be added separately rather than pretending one ruleset fits every market.
