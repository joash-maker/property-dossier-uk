The admin problem

The information is usually there. It just arrives in the wrong shape.

A vendor or landlord rarely sends a perfect CRM record. Details arrive across emails, follow-ups, attachments and conversations. Someone still has to read it, decide what matters, copy it across, chase the gaps and keep the record straight.

Information arrives in stages

Rent today. EPC tomorrow. Access instructions later. The property record gets patched together over time.

Staff re-key the same facts

Details are copied from email into CRM fields, notes, portal records and internal checklists.

Missing details are easy to overlook

Long emails can contain twenty useful facts. A few missed items become tomorrow’s chasing.

How it works

Import. Check. Request. Review. Hand over.

01

Import the property email

Paste a vendor or landlord email, or import a marked message through the configured Gmail connection.

02

Extract useful facts

Property, contact, rent or price, EPC, council tax, deposit, access and other details are proposed for review.

03

Request what is missing

Select missing details and conflicting values. Create one editable request to send through your agency email account.

04

Review the evidence

Paste the reply, compare the proposed changes with the source, resolve conflicting values and check the facts.

05

Hand off to the CRM

Preview, copy or download a reviewed internal summary. Outstanding supporting checks remain clearly labelled.

FAQ

The obvious questions.

We already have a CRM. Why would we need this?

Because the Studio handles the unstructured information before the CRM record is ready. It is designed to reduce the reading, copying, checking and chasing that still happens around the CRM.

Does it verify legal compliance?

No. It records what has been supplied, what source it came from and what the agent has reviewed. Official, legal and document checks remain separate and the agency keeps responsibility for professional judgement.

Does it only work with Gmail?

No. You can paste an email from your usual mailbox. The current build supports pasted emails and a configured Gmail marked-email connection. Other mailbox integrations are not included yet.

Does it update my CRM automatically?

No. The current handoff uses a copy-ready or downloadable text summary and a structured JSON export. Your team transfers the reviewed record. Direct CRM integrations are not included.

Does reviewed mean independently verified?

No. Review records that staff checked the information against the supplied source. A statement in an email remains a statement from its sender. Official or document verification needs its own evidence.

Does the app send requests for me?

No. It creates an editable draft for your team to copy or download. You send it through your agency email account and record that it was sent.

Can I hand over a record while some details are missing?

Yes, after the identity checks, conflict resolution and staff approval. The summary labels supporting gaps and review states. Marketing pack creation has a separate, fuller checklist and approval.

Can it handle lettings and property management?

Yes. The current workflow supports lettings information such as deposit, furnishing, availability, gas safety, EICR, maintenance authority, stopcock, fuse board and access instructions.

Is this only for the UK?

The current pilot is configured for England. Country-specific property workflows can be added separately rather than pretending one ruleset fits every market.
